What You Get

A Digest, Funder Pages, And A Tracker

Big tools for small nonprofits. Focus on your mission while FundBug finds the grants and funding partners aligned with your goals and sends them to you. No more hours searching massive databases. Includes application tracking, roadmaps and drafting support, and detailed funder pages.

Your Funding Digest

An email on the 1st and 3rd Tuesday of every month, in three parts:

  • New Matches: up to five Opportunities, programs with their own guidelines and deadlines.
  • Coming Up: deadlines you saved that fall in the next 30 days.
  • Funders To Know: up to five foundations to approach directly.

Every entry has a full write-up of why it fits, drawn from the program's own words and your organization's profile.

Funder Pages

A page for each foundation, built from its IRS 990-PF and Schedule I filings and its own website:

  • Giving by year, its typical grant, and its first grants to new grantees.
  • Where it gives, with its grantee list filtered to your county, your state, or all states.
  • The subjects of the organizations it funds.
  • How to apply as filed, the contact on file, and the pages from its own website.

Your Tracker

Save any match from your digest or your portal with one click, and its deadline shows up in Coming Up.

  • Keep what you are applying to in one place between digests.
  • Mark a listing Not A Fit and tell us why, so your matches improve.
  • Foundations that do not accept unsolicited applications never appear in your digest.

Your portal holds it all: your Matches, your Tracker, every funder page, past digests, your Organization Profile, and your answers to the questions foundations ask most.

Applying

From Match To Submitted

When a funder fits, FundBug helps you get the application ready.

01
See What It Takes
For any funder or program you save, an Application Roadmap lays out the documents to gather, the key dates, how to apply and who can apply, from its guidelines and, for a foundation, the instructions in its tax filing.
02
Bring In The Application
Upload it, paste its web address, or paste the text. FundBug pulls out the questions you have to write, what to attach and the dates, and you confirm them.
03
Draft Your Answers
FundBug drafts each answer from your Organization Profile, with placeholders where it needs your details, or outlines it if you would rather write it yourself. Save the good ones and reuse them on the next application.

Roadmaps and drafts are a starting point. A funder can ask for things it has not published, so read every answer and check the funder's current instructions before you submit.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Where Do The Funders Come From?

From two places: the tax filings foundations send the IRS each year (Form 990-PF, and Schedule I for public charities that make grants), and the funders' own websites. The filings show who a foundation funded, how much and where, how it says to apply, and the contact it gives. Its website adds its current guidelines and programs.

How Is This Different From A Grant Database?

A grant database waits for you to search it. FundBug does the searching: twice a month it sends the programs and funders that fit your organization, each with a write-up of why it fits and how to approach it. Its funder pages show what you would otherwise piece together from years of tax filings, such as what a foundation gives an organization it has not funded before, how much of its giving stays in your county, and the subjects of the organizations it funds.

Which States Do You Cover?

Massachusetts first, starting in the Berkshires, then New York. Organizations in any state can sign up, but coverage is deepest in Massachusetts.

Who Is FundBug For?

Community nonprofits, especially small and mid-sized ones without a full-time grants team, that look for grants from foundations and funding programs.

Will I See Foundations That Do Not Take Applications?

No. A foundation that does not accept unsolicited applications never appears in your digest.

What Happens After The Trial?

Your first 14 days are free. If you keep FundBug, your plan starts when the trial ends: $45 a month or $450 a year, whichever you chose. You add a card when you sign up, and it is not charged until the trial ends. Cancel before the trial ends and you pay nothing.

Does FundBug Write Our Applications?

It drafts them with you. FundBug writes first drafts of the questions foundations ask most, and of each application's own questions, from your Organization Profile, leaving a placeholder wherever it needs a detail only you know. Prefer to write in your own words? Choose outlines instead. You edit and approve everything, and FundBug never submits anything for you.

Does FundBug Guarantee I Will Be Funded?

No. FundBug finds funders and programs that fit your work and helps you prepare, but funding decisions belong to each funder, and we cannot promise any grant. Our information comes from funders' own pages and tax filings, which can change, and a funder may ask applicants for things it has not published. Always check a funder's current guidelines before you apply.

Can I Cancel Anytime?

Yes. Cancel anytime from Manage Billing in your portal.
