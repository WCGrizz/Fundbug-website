3.Applications, Roadmaps and the Browser Extension

Application Roadmaps. An Application Roadmap summarizes what we have read about how to apply to a program or funder: documents to gather, key dates, steps, who can apply, and the questions it asks. It is built from the program's or funder's published guidelines, its tax filings, and the pages and documents we have read, as of the date shown on it. A funder may require things it has not published, may change its requirements at any time, and pages we read may describe a past cycle. A Roadmap is not a complete or authoritative list of requirements. Where a Roadmap and the funder's own instructions differ, the funder's instructions control.

Applications you provide. When you upload an application, paste its text, or give us its web address, we retrieve and read it at your request to find its questions and requirements. You must have the right to share anything you upload. When you add, scan or upload an application for a funder or program we do not yet list, we may review its public details (such as the funder's name, web address and deadline) to consider adding it to our database. We do not publish or share your answers.

Browser extension. The FundBug browser extension reads the application form on the page you are viewing, only when you open it on that page, and fills fields with information from your FundBug profile and your saved answers only when you click to fill. It fills only empty fields. It does not fill passwords, payment, bank or identity-number fields, file uploads, signatures or agreements, and it never submits a form. Review every field before you submit. Application forms belong to third parties; FundBug is not responsible for how a third-party site stores, processes or handles what you submit to it. How the extension handles information is described in our Privacy Policy.
