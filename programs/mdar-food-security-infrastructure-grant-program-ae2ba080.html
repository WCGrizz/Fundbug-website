About this program

MDAR's Food Security Infrastructure Grant funds $10,000 to $500,000 projects that strengthen the Massachusetts food system, with a 20% cash match (in-kind not accepted) and reimbursement within 45 days of submission. The applicant list is unusually broad for MDAR: farms, seafood and aquaculture operations including commercial fishermen and dealers, food hubs and processors, food banks and pantries, schools and healthcare institutions, grocery stores, farmers markets, municipalities, and Tribal and Native-led non-profits. Projects must fall primarily within one of five types, from expanding local production to food system resilience, and priority goes to projects directly benefiting underserved communities.

Official program page: mass.gov ↗

Who is eligible?

To be eligible, applicants must: Be located and operating in Massachusetts; Have operated for at least three (3) consecutive years; Operate or support a commercial or noncommercial food operation that contributes to the Commonwealth's food system and serves Massachusetts residents.

The gate that catches people: equipment cannot be financed or leased, it must be fully paid for and owned before reimbursement, and deposits don't count. Farms without cash on hand typically pair FSIG with bridge financing (Berkshire Agricultural Ventures lends at 0% for exactly this) rather than vendor financing.

Not eligible if

  • An active FSIG contract already in place
  • Principal place of business outside Massachusetts
  • Under 3 consecutive years operating at the application deadline
  • Financed or leased equipment (equipment must be owned outright before reimbursement)
  • Deposits and costs incurred before the contract
