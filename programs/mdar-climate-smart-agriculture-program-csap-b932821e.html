About this program

The Climate Smart Agriculture Program is five formerly separate MDAR grants (food safety, environmental, climate resiliency, energy, and composting) merged into one application that can cover multiple project areas at once. MDAR reimburses 80% of project cost: established farms (3+ years) up to $50,000 per project area and $150,000 total, early-stage farms (1 to 3 years with $10,000+ in gross sales) up to $10,000 per area and $30,000 total. The 20% match must be cash per project area; in-kind contributions, donations, and other state grant funds don't count. For a small diversified farm, this is the largest realistic funding opportunity in the MDAR catalog.

Official program page: mass.gov ↗

Who is eligible?

Eligible applicants are Massachusetts farmers who operate an agriculture business that have been in continuous operation for a minimum of three (3) years to the date of application on the same property. Applicants that have been operating an agricultural business for a period of one to three years, and have annual gross sales of at least $10,000 may be eligible for reduced funding amounts.

Plan around the reimbursement structure: you pay first and MDAR reimburses 80% after completion, and anything spent before the contract is signed is ineligible. That cash-flow gap is what 0% bridge loans from lenders like Berkshire Agricultural Ventures exist to cover.

Not eligible if

  • Costs incurred before an executed MDAR contract (nothing is reimbursable before signing)
  • Under 3 years on the same property for the established tier (a recently relocated farm may not qualify despite longer history)
  • Used equipment, vehicles, land acquisition, drones, tractors and loaders, routine maintenance, or operating expenses
  • Leased sites without a lease running at least 5 years from application
