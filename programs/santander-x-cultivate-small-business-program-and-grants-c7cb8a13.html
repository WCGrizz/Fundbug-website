About this program

Santander's Cultivate Small Business is a free business education program for early-stage small businesses, delivered in two cohorts a year. Graduates are eligible for grant awards on completion. Santander's published eligibility is straightforward: at least one year in operation, working full time in the business, fewer than 10 total full-time equivalent employees, revenue between $25,000 and $1,000,000 in the last calendar year, and located in the United States.

The part worth planning around is the lead time. The fall cohort runs from September through November, with applications accepted March through June. The spring cohort runs March through June, with applications accepted November through January. In other words, the application window for any given cohort closes roughly three months before that cohort starts, which means the moment a business is most likely to hear about the program is the moment it is least able to join it.

A note on how we corrected this page. An earlier version of this page said applications were open, and gave specific cohort dates and award amounts. If you are evaluating this program, confirm the current award amounts and any industry requirements with Santander directly.

Who is eligible?

Cultivate Small Business supports entrepreneurs and businesses that meet the following criteria:

In operation for at least one year

Are working full time in the business and have fewer than 10 total full-time equivalent employees

Revenues between $25,000 and $1,000,000 in the last calendar year

Located in the United States

Our Fall cohort runs from September through November with applications accepted from March through June.

Our Spring cohort runs from March through June with applications accepted from November through January.

The criteria above are quoted from Santander's own program page. This program has historically focused on food businesses; Santander's currently published criteria do not state an industry restriction, so confirm before applying.

Not eligible if

  • Under one year in operation
  • Revenue outside the $25,000 to $1,000,000 range
  • 10 or more full-time-equivalent employees
  • Owner not working full time in the business
  • Located outside the United States
