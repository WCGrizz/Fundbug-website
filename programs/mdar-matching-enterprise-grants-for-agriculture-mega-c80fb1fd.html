About this program

MDAR's Matching Enterprise Grants for Agriculture awards Massachusetts farms $15,000, $20,000, or $30,000 with a 1:1 cash match, aimed at farms in their first one to ten years with farm income showing on filed taxes. The match can come from savings, loans, or non-MDAR grants, but not sweat equity, existing assets, or money already spent, and it only has to be documented before the contract, not at application. Business planning and technical assistance are free whether or not a grant follows, which makes applying worthwhile even for a farm still deciding on its capital project.

Official program page: mass.gov ↗

Who is eligible?

How do I know if I am considered a new farmer for the purposes of this program?You must have been in business for at least 1 year but no more than 10 years to apply. You should have filed taxes for your farm business for at least the prior year to that show that you are producing and selling farm products. A copy of the prior year tax form showing Gross Farm Income (Schedule F or other appropriate tax form, depending on business structure) must be submitted along with the application by the application deadline. An existing farm business would not be considered an eligible beginning farm due to changes in legal business entity, business name, or farming location.

I need money to start my farm business. Am I eligible to apply?

No. To apply you must have access to land, have already started and have been operating your farm business for at least one year.

I have been farming for more than 10 years and want to start a new business enterprise on my farm. Can I apply for a MEGA grant for the new business?

No. The MEGA Program is for farmers who have been operating their own commercial farm business for at least 1 but no more than 10 years. It is not for new enterprises on existing farms (that have been in business more than 10 years).

Our farm is operated as a non-profit organization. Are we able to apply?

No. Non-profit farms and non-profit organizations are not eligible to apply to MEGA.

We own 20 acres and have been in business for 4 years. Should we apply to MEGA or Farm Viability?

Farmers who own more than 5 acres of agricultural land and have operated for three years or more may consider applying to the Farm Viability Enhancement Program (FVEP) if they meet program eligibility criteria. If your farm income/sales have been modest, or your land base limited, MEGA may be a better choice than Farm Viability. Should you choose to apply to MEGA and are awarded a grant, you may apply to participate in FVEP for additional assistance once 3 years has passed from the start date of your MEGA contract.

My family received a Farm Viability grant a few years ago and I now want to start a new business on their farm. Can I apply for a MEGA grant?

If your family’s farm has participated in the Farm Viability Program previously, you are not eligible to apply for a MEGA grant for improvements to take place on the same farm property.

Must I have a certain number of acres to apply?

There is no minimum acreage required to apply to MEGA. However, it is the objective of the Program to assist farmers in developing commercially viable operations. If you are farming on very small acreage, you may wish to explain why size will not be a deterrent to viability.

I rent my farmland. Am I eligible to apply?

You do not need to own your farmland to apply. However, you do need to have secure access to the land. That means you must have a written lease agreement that provides a legal right to use the property. You will need to provide a copy of your signed lease along with your application by the application deadline, and the landowner must sign the MEGA application (along with the applicant). If selected to participate in the program, the term of the lease agreement must be for at least three (3) years from the contract start date in order to proceed to a grant contract. Depending on the length of the participant’s current lease, this may require a new lease to be signed before a Phase II contract can be executed.

I live in Massachusetts but my farmland is across the border in another state. Am I eligible to apply?

No. To apply, your farm must be located in Massachusetts.

I completed an application last year but never sent it in. Can I submit that application now?

No, you will need to complete a new current application form. The applications are revised each year, and the Request for Response may contain changes to program requirements from the prior year.

MEGA or Farm Viability? MDAR's own guidance: more than 5 owned acres plus 3+ years operating points to the Farm Viability Enhancement Program; a modest income or small land base points to MEGA. A MEGA recipient can apply to FVEP three years after the MEGA contract starts.

Not eligible if

  • Farm operating more than 10 years (including a new enterprise on an older farm)
  • Non-profit farms
  • Property that previously received an FVEP grant
  • Renters without a written lease running 3+ years from contract start
  • Operating expenses, debt, livestock, owner or employee labor, or land purchases
  • Costs incurred before the contract is executed
