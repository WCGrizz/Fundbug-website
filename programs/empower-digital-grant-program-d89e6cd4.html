About this program

The Empower Digital Grant was an MGCC matching grant that helped Massachusetts small businesses fund technology and digital marketing: websites, e-commerce, point-of-sale systems, cybersecurity, and digital marketing services, accessed through the state's Small Business Technical Assistance network. With MGCC's absorption into MassDevelopment, the program is no longer offered, though MassDevelopment continues to run small business grant programs of its own.

Official program page: massdevelopment.com ↗

Who is eligible?

Open to any eligible business type at any stage located in Massachusetts; funds restricted to technology and marketing uses.
