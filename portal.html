Your story

This shapes the match explanations in your digest, and doubles as the starting point for application drafts, so write it the way you'd want a funder to read it. Include the details that make you and your business unique, your story is often what sets an application apart.

Any recent changes to your business, goals, or capital needs that we should factor into your matches.
How you operate

Where and how you run the business. Owning your space unlocks facade, building, and real-estate improvement grants; seasonal and online details surface the right specialty programs.
