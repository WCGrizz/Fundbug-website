About FundBug

Built in the Berkshires, for the places that keep a community going

I've built FundBug for the small businesses and organizations that are the keystone of local communities, in the Berkshires and elsewhere.

Hi, I'm Will Corridan.

I live and work here in the Berkshires, and FundBug is my company. I built it for the businesses and nonprofits that do so much for the places they serve.

The money is out there: grants, loans, tax credits, rebates, and foundations that give every year. Finding the right ones is the hard part. It means checking dozens of websites, reading the fine print and keeping track of deadlines, usually on top of a full-time job. FundBug does that searching for you, so your time goes to the work itself.

I built FundBug myself. It started as a passion project and quickly turned into something bigger. I saw a gap: AI tools weren't serving Main Street America, and I wanted to help people chase their dreams.

I still review the listings before they reach you. If something looks wrong, or you have a question, write to me at hello@fundbug.net. I read every email.

What FundBug does

For Small Businesses

Twice a month, a short digest of the grants, loans, tax credits and rebates your business can actually get, ranked by how well you fit. Each one says in plain English why it fits you.

FundBug for small businesses →

For Nonprofits

Twice a month, the grants and funders that fit your work, drawn from funders' own guidelines and their tax filings, so you can see what each foundation gives, where, and how much.

FundBug for nonprofits →

How it works

  1. Tell us about yourself, once.What you do, where you work, and what you're working toward. Your profile is how every program gets matched to you.
  2. FundBug matches every program against it.Listings come from official sources: agencies' and funders' own pages, and foundations' tax filings. A person checks each one before it goes live.
  3. Your digest arrives on the 1st and 3rd Tuesday.Everything also lands in your portal, with a tracker for what you're applying to and help drafting your applications.

Questions?

Write to hello@fundbug.net or use the contact page. I answer every email myself.
