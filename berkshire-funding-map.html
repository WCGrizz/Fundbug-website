Berkshire County

Who to call, for what

A plain-language map of which organization in Berkshire County helps with what: advice, capital, hiring, and funding.

Coming soon. This page is being reviewed by the organizations it describes, so that every entry reflects how each of them would describe their own work rather than how we guessed at it. It will be published once that is done.

If you run a business in Berkshire County and you need help right now, the fastest starting point is usually the Massachusetts Small Business Development Center's Berkshire office. Their advising is free and confidential, and if they are not the right fit for your question they will know who is.

How FundBug helps
We watch the funding landscape so you don't have to.

FundBug tracks grants, specialized loans, rebates, tax credits and free programs across Massachusetts and Rhode Island, down to the town level, and re-verifies them on a schedule. Tell us about your business once, and twice a month we send you the opportunities you actually qualify for.
